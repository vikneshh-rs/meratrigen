Advancing Precision Medicine through Genomic Insights

Modern medicine is transitioning from a one-size-fits-all approach to precision medicine, where prevention, diagnosis, and treatment are tailored to an individual's genetic makeup. We harness Whole Exome Sequencing (WES) and Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS) across clinical and academic settings.

Whole Exome Sequencing (WES)
Sequences all protein-coding regions (exons) of the genome — about 1-2% of the genome that harbors nearly 85% of known disease-causing mutations.
Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS)
Comprehensive analysis of the entire genome, including coding and non-coding regions, structural variants, and regulatory elements.
